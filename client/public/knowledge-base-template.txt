KNOWLEDGE BASE TEMPLATE
=======================

How to use this template:
- Replace everything in [square brackets] with your own information.
- Delete any section that doesn't apply to your business. A short, accurate file works better than a long one.
- Write in plain sentences, the way you'd explain it to a new employee. Keep each fact in the section it belongs to.
- Don't add rules about how the assistant should talk. Answering briefly, staying on topic and pointing people to you when it doesn't know are already built in.
- Your contact details, hours, location and top FAQs can go in the Business Profile on your admin page instead. The assistant always knows those.
- Don't include anything private: no passwords, internal pricing, staff phone numbers or customer details.
- When you're done, save as .txt (or .docx / .pdf) and upload it on your admin page. Delete these instructions first.
