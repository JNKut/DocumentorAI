APPLECARE & WARRANTY

Standard Warranty:
- All Apple devices include a 1-year Apple Limited Warranty
- 90 days of complimentary technical support included
- Apple Watch Edition and Hermès include 2-year warranty

AppleCare+:
- Extends warranty coverage beyond the standard 1 year
- Includes 24/7 priority tech support
- Unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection (service fee applies)
- AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss: up to 2 incidents of theft/loss every 12 months
- Must be purchased within 60 days of device purchase
- AppleCare One (multi-device): starts at $19.99/month; add devices for $5.99/month each

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I contact Apple Support?
A: Call 1-800-692-7753, visit support.apple.com, use the Apple Support app, or visit an Apple Store.

Q: What is AppleCare+?
A: An extended warranty plan that adds accidental damage coverage, 24/7 priority support, and optional theft/loss protection. Must be purchased within 60 days of your device purchase.

Q: Can I return my iPhone if I don't like it?
A: Yes. Apple offers a 14-day return policy for items in original condition purchased from Apple directly.

Q: Does Apple offer student discounts?
A: Yes. Apple Education Pricing is available to students, teachers, and staff. Visit apple.com/education for details.

Q: Where can I buy Apple products?
A: At apple.com, Apple retail stores, authorized resellers like Best Buy, or by calling 1-800-692-7753.

Q: How long does shipping take from Apple?com?
A: Standard shipping is typically 3-5 business days. Express options are available at checkout.

Q: What is Apple One?
A: A bundle subscription that combines Apple TV+, Apple Music, Apple Arcade, and iCloud+ at a reduced price compared to buying separately.
